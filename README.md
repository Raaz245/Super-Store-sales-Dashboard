## 🎯 Objective

To analyze sales, profit, and order data from a US-based retail superstore and provide business insights through interactive visualizations — helping stakeholders make data-driven decisions.

---

## 📈 Key Business Questions Answered

- Which **regions and states** drive the most revenue and profit?
- Which **product categories and sub-categories** are most profitable?
- How do **sales and profit trend over time** (monthly/quarterly)?
- Which **customer segments** contribute most to overall sales?
- What is the **average discount impact** on profit margins?

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- 📦 **Technology** is the highest revenue-generating category
- 📉 **Furniture** shows high sales but low profit margins due to heavy discounting
- 🗺️ **West and East regions** lead in total sales contribution
- 👤 **Consumer segment** accounts for ~50% of total orders
- 📅 Sales peak in **Q4** (Oct–Dec), indicating seasonal demand

## 🛠️ Dashboard Features

- ✅ Dynamic **date slicer** for time-period filtering
- ✅ **Region and segment** filter panel
- ✅ KPI cards — Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, Avg Discount
- ✅ Bar chart — Sales by Sub-Category
- ✅ Line chart — Monthly Sales Trend
- ✅ Map visual — Sales by State
- ✅ Donut chart — Sales by Segment
